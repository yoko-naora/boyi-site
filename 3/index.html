Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #3: 借刀杀人 (jiè dāo shā rén), "Borrow a knife to kill a person."

The story

Over 2,700 years ago, the ruler of Zheng wanted the land of his small neighbor, Kuai. Kuai had good ministers and good generals. So he didn't send soldiers. He wrote their names on a list, promised each one a title and farmland, and buried the list outside Kuai's city gate like a secret treaty. The lord of Kuai found it, and did the rest himself.

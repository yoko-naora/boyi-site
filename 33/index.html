Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #33: 反间计 (fǎn jiàn jì), "Turn the spy".

The story

Yan's general Yue Yi has spent years winning city after city in Qi, until only two remain. The old king of Yan dies, and the new king never liked Yue Yi. Tian Dan, who holds one of the last cities, sends word to Yan that Yue Yi wants to stay and rule Qi, and that what Qi fears most is Yan replacing him. The new king believes it, sends a replacement general and recalls Yue Yi, who fears for his life and goes west to Zhao. The Yan army loses heart, and Tian Dan drives it back and wins back Qi's cities. The two Records of the Grand Historian chapters differ slightly, so we follow the Biography of Yue Yi and say so. Whether Yue Yi really wanted a crown, both books call the whole thing a rumor, not a fact.

In this video

  • The story of Tian Dan and Yue Yi, with the two chapters' small differences noted
  • A modern sales team: a new CEO, a rumor in an industry newsletter, and a rival who won the clients back
  • What to do before acting on a rumor: speak to the person, check the source, ask who gains, keep a written record

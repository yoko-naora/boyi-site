Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #11: 李代桃僵 (lǐ dài táo jiāng), "A plum takes the peach's fall."

The story

A famous Chinese play: The Orphan of Zhao. A powerful minister wipes out one family. One baby is left. A country doctor hides the baby in his medicine box and carries him out of the gate — then hands over his own son to keep the real child alive.

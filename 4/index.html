Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #4: 以逸待劳 (yǐ yì dài láo), "Wait at ease for the tired enemy."

The story

More than 2,200 years ago, General Li Mu held Zhao's northern border. Raiders came every year. His one order: if they come, everyone goes inside the walls. Nobody fights. Everyone called him a coward, even his own king. But behind the walls he fed his men, drilled them, paid scouts and kept the beacons lit. When the raiders finally came with everything they had, he was ready.

Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #9: 隔岸观火 (gé àn guān huǒ), "Watch the fire from the other bank."

The story

Two brothers had lost a war and a kingdom, and ran east with a few thousand riders. The man who beat them did not chase them to the end. His officers wanted one more campaign. He said no army. "He will send them to me himself." In the ninth month he turned around and went home. The two brothers reached the far province and asked to stay. The man inside had one question: if the capital comes, do these two stand with me, or hand me over? Either answer ended badly for him. So he solved it before the capital arrived. Nobody fought for them. Then a messenger rode west, walked into the tent, said one sentence, and every officer turned around. His reason was two lines: that man had always been afraid of these two brothers; push him, and he has to stand with them; leave him alone, and he does it himself.

Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #29: 树上开花 (shù shàng kāi huā), "Make flowers bloom on a bare tree".

The story

The old account behind this stratagem is in the Records of the Three Kingdoms. Nearly 1,800 years ago, Liu Bei's family and followers are fleeing before a pursuing army, and Zhang Fei is left behind with twenty horsemen to hold the rear. He stands at the water, takes up his spear, gives his name, and calls out for anyone who dares to fight him to the death. No one in the pursuing army dares come near. Liu Bei escapes. A later novel adds that Zhang Fei also had branches dragged to raise dust. That detail is fiction, not history. The point of the old account is borrowed momentum: a small force looks big because of the ground it stands on.

Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #2: 围魏救赵 (wéi Wèi jiù Zhào), "Besiege Wei to rescue Zhao."

The story

354 BC. The kingdom of Wei surrounds Handan, capital of Zhao. Zhao begs Qi for help. Everyone expects Qi's army to march straight to Handan. Its strategist, Sun Bin, says no, and sends the army to the one place the enemy can't ignore.

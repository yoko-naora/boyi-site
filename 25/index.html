Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #25: 偷梁换柱 (tōu liáng huàn zhù), "Secretly swap the beams for pillars".

The story

The old account behind this stratagem comes from the Records of the Grand Historian. More than two thousand years ago, the First Emperor of Qin falls ill on a tour of the empire and has a letter sealed for his eldest son. The letter never leaves the hands of the man who keeps the imperial seals. When the emperor dies, his chief minister agrees to keep the death secret, and the court carries on as usual. Then the seal-keeper persuades the younger son, and then the minister himself, and a new letter is written and stamped with the real seal. The seal is real. The court looks unchanged. Only the one thing that mattered was swapped.

Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #31: 美人计 (měi rén jì), "The beautiful woman stratagem".

The story

This stratagem's famous story is not in the Records of the Grand Historian. It comes from later books, the Spring and Autumn Annals of Wu and Yue and the Yuejue Shu, and mixes history with legend, so we tell it as "later accounts say". Yue has been beaten by Wu. A minister of Yue lists nine ways to weaken Wu. The fourth is to send beautiful women to cloud the king's judgment, because Wu's king is said to be fond of beauty. Yue finds Xi Shi, the daughter of a woodcutter, and trains her for three years before offering her to Wu. The king of Wu is delighted. His adviser Wu Zixu warns that a beautiful woman can bring a state to ruin. The king does not listen, and has Wu Zixu sent a sword. The Records of the Grand Historian give a different reason for that death, a quarrel over a war and a rival's slander, with no beauty in it. What the king of Wu did with Xi Shi next, the books do not say, so we do not invent it.

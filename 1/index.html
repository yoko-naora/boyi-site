Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #1: 瞒天过海 (mán tiān guò hǎi), "Hide it from heaven, cross the sea."

The story

In 589 AD, a Sui general named He Ruobi didn't hide his army. He showed it, again and again, in full view, until the other side stopped looking. Then he crossed the river.

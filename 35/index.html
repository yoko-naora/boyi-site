Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #35: 连环计 (lián huán jì), "The linked-rings strategy".

The story

Dong Zhuo holds the court and cannot be opposed openly, so the minister Wang Yun first treats Lü Bu, Dong Zhuo's guard, well. The two men already have a rift of their own: Dong Zhuo once hurled a halberd at Lü Bu, and Lü Bu fears being found out over a maid. Lü Bu comes to Wang Yun himself. He says "but we are like father and son," and Wang Yun answers, "Your name is Lü. You are not his flesh and blood." At the palace gate, Lü Bu kills Dong Zhuo. Afterward Wang Yun looks down on Lü Bu and moves to dismiss Dong Zhuo's old Liangzhou troops; rumors spread that all Liangzhou people will be killed, those generals attack the capital, and Wang Yun is killed. The name Diaochan and the love story are from the novel Romance of the Three Kingdoms; the histories say only "a maid."

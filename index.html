Read the 36 Stratagems with Yoko

I'm Yoko. I was born in Chengdu, Sichuan — the old Kingdom of Shu from Romance of the Three Kingdoms — and these days I live in Tokyo. I'll walk you through the stories every Chinese kid grows up with: the 36 Stratagems, Romance of the Three Kingdoms, The Art of War, and more.我是阳子，出生在四川成都，就是《三国演义》里的蜀国，现在住在东京。我会给你讲中国人从小就懂的三十六计、从小就读的《三国演义》，还有《孙子兵法》等等。

Each episode starts with the old story. Then we talk about where that same move shows up today — at work, between husband and wife, in a family fight — and how you can actually use it.每一计先讲古代的故事，再聊它今天出现在哪儿——办公室、夫妻之间、家里吵架的时候——我们怎么用。

You don't have to memorize every one. Just keep watching, keep trying them out, and you'll start to see how much Chinese wisdom can help you sort things out.不用每一计都记住，反复看、反复用，你会发现中国人的智慧能帮你解决很多问题。

And one more thing: if you want to make Chinese friends, try dropping one of the 36 stratagems into the conversation. They might just see you in a whole new light.最后，想和中国人交朋友？说出三十六计里的一计，也许他会对你刮目相看！

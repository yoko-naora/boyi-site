Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #5: 趁火打劫 (chèn huǒ dǎ jié), "Take what you can while the house burns."

The story

More than 2,500 years ago, King Fuchai of Wu marched his best soldiers north to a meeting of lords, to be named first among them. Back home: a bad harvest, and only the old and the weak. In the south, King Goujian of Yue waited one more year. Then his adviser said one word: now. Yue never lit the first fire. It just walked in after it started.

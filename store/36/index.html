Questions

Before you buy

How does the early-bird price work?
The collection is being finished one stratagem at a time. While it is in progress the price is $12. The regular price is $19.
What happens when a new stratagem comes out?
It is added to the collection, and to your copy. You pay once — the $12 covers all 36, including the ones that do not exist yet.
Do the free PDFs stay free?
Yes. Every stratagem keeps its free PDF on the site, exactly as it is today. The collection does not take anything away from the free pages.
How do I pay?
Not yet. Payments are not open — this page is a preview. Nothing is charged, and no email address or card details are collected.
How do I get the audio?
When you buy, your receipt email carries a private download page. Each track plays right there with one press, and “Download all” takes the whole set at once — the PDF and the MP3s together — on a phone or on a computer. If you bought at the early-bird price, that same page keeps collecting the new tracks as they are recorded.
Can I get a refund?
Yes — within 7 days of purchase, for any reason. Email us and we'll refund in full.
Which stratagems are already out?
Twelve, from #1 瞒天过海 to #12 顺手牵羊. The free pages are on the main site, one per stratagem.

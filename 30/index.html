Every Chinese kid learns the 36 Stratagems. This is #30: 反客为主 (fǎn kè wéi zhǔ), "Turn the guest into the host".

The story

The old account behind this stratagem is in the Records of the Three Kingdoms. Nearly 1,800 years ago, Liu Zhang, the ruler of Yi Province, fears a stronger army will take his land, and invites Liu Bei in to fight the rival to the north. He welcomes him, sends gifts, and adds troops, until Liu Bei commands more than thirty thousand. At a banquet, an adviser urges Liu Bei to seize the province. He answers: "This is a major matter. It cannot be rushed." He goes north and does not attack the rival. Instead he is recorded as "building up goodwill to win the people's hearts." The record does not say whether this was his plan from the start, so we do not guess. When he asks Liu Zhang for troops and supplies, he gets a fraction, and a letter from one of Liu Zhang's own advisers is exposed. Relations break. Liu Bei marches south, besieges the capital, and Liu Zhang surrenders.
